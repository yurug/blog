Selected talks and conversations by Yann Régis-Gianas on agentic software development, Tezos, programming languages, and formal methods.

I speak about the same boundary I study in this blog: how first-principles reasoning and formal guarantees meet engineering systems operated by people.

Stop pedaling: First principles for spec-driven agentic development#

TezDev 2026, Cannes. A practical argument for moving engineering effort from generating more code toward specifications, checks, knowledge, and the loop around the agent.
